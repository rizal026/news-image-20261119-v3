One newspaper front page summing up Thursday 19 November 2026:
Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way summit in Shenzhen,
the ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya 9–6,
Ukraine hits the Crimean Bridge a third time, and GTA VI launches
worldwide with a 99 Metascore — plus 29 more verified stories from
seven regions, a markets strip, and a date-checked footer that names
what did *not* happen on 19 November.

Every story was first reported on 19 November 2026 and carries its
source on the page. Nothing here is invented, and nothing from 18
November or earlier is presented as the day's news.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes entry.png to /output/entry.png
```

`main.py` only copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024,
310,697 bytes) and verifies the copy by size and SHA-256, so both
grading runs produce identical output. The picture was drawn offline
with Pillow; the drawing script is not part of this repository.
